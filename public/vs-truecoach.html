Common questions

TrueCoach concerns answered

I am worried about the Xplor acquisition. Should I be? +
That concern is legitimate. Xplor acquired TrueCoach in 2022, and since then the product roadmap has become less transparent, prices have adjusted, and AI features that were independent are now framed as part of Xplor's broader platform. When a PE-backed platform acquires a coaching tool, the incentives shift from “serve coaches well” to “drive ARPU.” That is not a prediction — it is a structural reality worth factoring into your decision. FitOS is MIT-licensed open source: no acquirer, no roadmap surprises, no reliance on any single company remaining in business.
Can I export my data if I leave TrueCoach? +
TrueCoach offers some data export but it is limited — you can get CSVs of basic client info and program structures, but full messaging history, check-in photos, and workout logs do not export cleanly. With FitOS your data is in a Google Sheet and Google Drive you already own. There is nothing to export — it was always yours. Cancel your FitOS subscription and your Sheet, your photos, your client history all stay untouched in your own Google account.
What if TrueCoach raises prices again? +
TrueCoach's pricing has shifted since the Xplor acquisition. When you are on a subscription platform, you have two choices when prices rise: pay more or migrate under pressure. FitOS is a one-time license — you paid once, the software is yours. The only ongoing cost is the optional $7/mo managed hosting, which you can eliminate entirely by self-hosting. Price increases cannot touch you.
Is the 5-year cost comparison fair? What am I actually comparing? +
The default comparison is TrueCoach at $30/mo billed annually ($1,800 over 5 years) vs FitOS at $497 license + $7/mo hosted × 57 months after the 3 free months = $896 total. That is a saving of roughly $904. If you self-host FitOS, it is $497 once vs $1,800 — you keep $1,303. Use the slider above to adjust years and billing frequency to model your specific situation.
TrueCoach has better mobile apps. Does FitOS have a mobile app? +
FitOS ships a Progressive Web App (PWA) — clients install it from their browser on iOS or Android and it runs offline-first. It handles workouts, check-ins, history, and messages. It is not the same as a native app: no push notifications yet, slightly less polished than TrueCoach's dedicated apps. If native mobile is a firm requirement, TrueCoach wins that dimension clearly. If “installable app that works offline” is enough, FitOS covers it.
How does FitOS handle AI if it is not connected to Xplor's platform? +
FitOS uses Anthropic's Claude API with BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). You supply your own key, stored AES-256-GCM encrypted in your Google Sheet's Profile tab — never logged, never shared with us. The AI Program Builder generates full client programs from a single prompt. The skill runner lets you drop custom AI skills into the skills/ folder with no code changes. Your AI usage, your API spend, your control — no platform dependencies.
