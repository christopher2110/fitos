    Share with FitOS

    FitOS reads your Sheet using a service account. You need to share your Sheet with this email address and give it Editor access.

    Loading service account email…
    1. In your Google Sheet, click the Share button (top right)
    2. In the "Add people and groups" field, paste the email above
    3. Change the permission to Editor (not just Viewer)
    4. Click Send — uncheck "Notify people" if you don't want a bounce email
