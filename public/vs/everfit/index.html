Frequently Asked Questions

The questions coaches ask before they switch.

Can I migrate my clients from Everfit to FitOS?
Yes. Everfit lets you export client data. FitOS has a one-click importer that brings in your client roster in about 60 seconds — no manual re-entry. Start your migration →

The main work is reassigning active programs to the new platform, which most coaches time to coincide with a new training block. Your client history, check-ins, and metrics come with you.
Is FitOS as polished as Everfit?
Everfit has a genuinely strong UI — it's been polished over several years with VC funding. FitOS is built for performance, not flash. Coaches who care about data ownership, open-source flexibility, and lifetime pricing consistently choose FitOS over Everfit once they see what renting software actually costs over five years.

The 14-day free trial shows you exactly what you're getting. No card required.
What happens if Everfit raises prices?
You pay more or you leave — and leaving means rebuilding. Everfit's data portability is partial: client data exports fine, but programs must be rebuilt manually in the new platform. The switching cost is exactly why SaaS platforms can raise prices with minimal churn.

With FitOS, the license is lifetime. $497 is your total software cost, forever. The hosting ($7/mo on Vercel) is your only recurring charge, and you can move that anywhere.
Does Everfit have an API?
Yes, but it's gated. API access requires the Studio or Enterprise tier — it's not available on the Pro plan where most individual coaches operate. Zapier integration is also a paid add-on on top of your subscription.

FitOS is open source. The full API is yours. You can build any custom agent, automation, or third-party integration without hitting a paywall.
Is Everfit a US company?
Everfit Inc. operates as a US-registered entity, but is built and operated from Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. It's VC-backed with investors who have standard liquidity expectations. That's not inherently a problem, but it means pricing and feature decisions are made by people accountable to investors, not coaches.
What if Everfit shuts down?
If Everfit shuts down, your software access ends. Every client program, every history log, every customization — gone. VC-backed fitness SaaS companies have shut down before (see: Mindbody acqui-hire consolidations, Whoop platform changes).

With FitOS, you have the source code. Even if FitOS the company disappeared tomorrow, you can run the software yourself. That's what "ownership" means.
