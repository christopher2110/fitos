Before you start

The import tool writes directly to your connected Google Sheet. Make sure your sheet is connected before uploading files.

Prerequisites: You need a FitOS account (trial or paid) with a Google Sheet connected via the Setup wizard. The import tool is available at /dashboard/import.

The importer is idempotent: running the same file twice will not create duplicate clients (it checks by name and email). Workout rows are always appended.

Migrating from Trainerize

Trainerize lets you export two types of CSV: your client roster and your training log. Export both, then import them one at a time.

Step 1: Export your clients from Trainerize

1
Go to Settings → Business → Data

In your Trainerize web dashboard, open the Settings panel on the left sidebar.

2
Click "Export Clients"

Select the client group (All clients) and click Export. A CSV downloads to your computer.

Step 2: Export your Training Log

3
Go to Settings → Data → Training Log

Select a date range (recommended: All Time) and click Export. This produces the workout history CSV.

Step 3: Import into FitOS

4
Open /dashboard/import

Select Trainerize as your source. Upload the Clients CSV first (type auto-detected), then import the Training Log CSV.

Migrating from TrueCoach

TrueCoach (now owned by Xplor) supports CSV exports for your client list and your workout results.

Step 1: Export your clients

1
Go to Settings → Data Export

Select "Clients" and click Export. A CSV with your full client roster downloads.

Step 2: Export workout results

2
Go to Settings → Data Export → Results

Choose a date range and export. This CSV contains exercise completions with sets, reps, and weights.

Step 3: Import into FitOS

3
Open /dashboard/import

Select TrueCoach as your source and upload your files one at a time.

Using a generic CSV

If you're exporting from another tool, make sure your CSV headers include at least:

  • Clients: Name (or Full Name) and Email
  • Workouts: Date, Exercise, Sets, Reps

The importer accepts any spelling variations — it matches by substring, not exact name.

After the import

Once your data is in FitOS, here's what to do next:

1
Review your Clients tab in Google Sheets

Open your connected Sheet, click the Clients tab, and verify the imported rows look correct.

2
Build or assign programs

Use the AI Program Builder at /dashboard/agents/builder to generate programs for each client.

3
Auto-fill exercise video links

If you imported an exercise library, visit /dashboard/exercises and use Find Videos to auto-link YouTube demos.

4
Share the client PWA link

Your clients access their workouts at /workouts. Send them the link so they can log sessions from their phone.

FAQ

Will importing duplicate my existing clients?

No. The importer checks existing client names and emails before writing. A client is skipped if their name or email already exists in your Clients tab.

What happens to workout history that is already in my Sheet?

Imported workout rows are appended to the Workouts tab. They do not overwrite anything. If you import the same file twice, you will get duplicate workout rows — but client imports are idempotent.

My CSV has different column names. Will it still work?

Yes, as long as the column names are close to the recognized variants. The importer matches by substring. If a column is not recognized, that field is left blank. You can always edit your Sheet directly after import.

What if the import fails?

The error message will tell you what went wrong. Common issues: Sheet not connected (go to /setup), CSV is not UTF-8 encoded, or the file type could not be detected (manually set it to Clients or Workouts).

Can I import programs and training plans?

Not yet. The Program tab has its own schema that doesn't map directly from Trainerize/TrueCoach exports. Use the AI Program Builder to recreate programs, or edit the Program tab in Google Sheets directly.
