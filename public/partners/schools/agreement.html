Section 1
Definitions

"FitOS" means the web-based personal training management software made available at fitos-zc11.polsia.app and any successor domains.

"Enrolled Student" means any individual registered in a curriculum program offered by School during the Term.

"Hosting Token" means a unique access credential issued to an Enrolled Student entitling them to use the FitOS hosted environment during enrollment and for thirty (30) days post-graduation.

"Graduation" means the date on which an Enrolled Student completes or withdraws from School's curriculum program.

"Alumni" means a graduate who chooses to continue using FitOS under a paid plan after Hosting Token expiry.

Section 2
Partnership Terms

2.1 License Grant. FitOS grants School a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to include FitOS in School's curriculum and to distribute Hosting Tokens to Enrolled Students for the duration of this Agreement.

2.2 Volume Pricing Tiers. The per-student license fee is determined by School's annual enrollment:

Annual Enrollment Price / Student Approx. Net Margin / Student
20–39 students$197~$150
40–79 students$187~$160
80–149 students$177~$165
150+ students$167~$167

2.3 Payment — Option A (Invoice). School will be invoiced quarterly based on actual Enrolled Students in that quarter. Payment due within fifteen (15) days of invoice date.

2.4 Payment — Option B (Revenue Share). At School's election, School may remit 70% of each student's FitOS fee at the time of tuition collection, retaining 30% as a distribution fee. Election must be made in writing before the start of each academic year.

2.5 Hosting Tokens. FitOS will issue Hosting Tokens within two (2) business days of School providing a valid student enrollment list. Tokens activate immediately and expire automatically thirty (30) days after the student's Graduation date. School is responsible for providing accurate enrollment and graduation dates.

Section 3
Co-Branding (Optional)

3.1 Upon written request, FitOS may add School's logo to the student-facing FitOS PWA splash screen and login page for students enrolled through School. FitOS branding and "Powered by FitOS" attribution must remain visible at all times.

3.2 FitOS will implement approved co-branding within ten (10) business days of receiving approved logo assets in SVG or PNG format ≥ 300px height.

3.3 Co-branding is additive only. School's logo may not replace or obscure FitOS branding. White-label arrangements (removal of FitOS identity) require a separate written agreement.

Section 4
Alumni Upgrade Program

4.1 Upon Hosting Token expiry, Enrolled Students (now Alumni) will receive an in-app offer to continue using FitOS at retail pricing: $497 lifetime license or $7/month subscription.

4.2 Alumni Revenue Share. If School elects Option B (Revenue Share) under Section 2.4, FitOS will also share twenty percent (20%) of any Alumni upgrade revenue attributable to School's graduates for a period of twenty-four (24) months from each Alumni's Graduation date. Attribution is tracked by enrollment token origin. Alumni revenue share is paid quarterly alongside school fees.

4.3 Schools electing Option A (Invoice) do not receive Alumni revenue share unless separately negotiated.

Section 5
Student Data & Privacy

5.1 FitOS software is licensed under the MIT License. Students own all data they enter into their FitOS instance. FitOS does not sell or share student data with third parties.

5.2 Upon Hosting Token expiry, student data is archived for ninety (90) days, after which it is deleted from FitOS servers. Students retain a data export option during this period.

5.3 School agrees to inform Enrolled Students, prior to enrollment, that their FitOS usage data (login frequency, feature usage metrics) may be aggregated anonymously by FitOS for product improvement purposes.

Section 6
Intellectual Property

6.1 The FitOS codebase is licensed under the MIT License. Students receive this license at enrollment and retain it after Graduation. School receives no ownership rights in FitOS software.

6.2 School may not resell, sublicense, or white-label FitOS without prior written consent from FitOS.

6.3 Any curriculum materials School creates referencing FitOS (guides, tutorials, assessments) remain School's property. School grants FitOS a non-exclusive license to reference the partnership in marketing materials.

Section 7
Term & Termination

7.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months, auto-renewing annually unless either party provides sixty (60) days written notice of non-renewal.

7.2 Either party may terminate for material breach upon thirty (30) days written notice if the breach is not cured within that period.

7.3 Upon termination, all active Hosting Tokens remain valid until their scheduled expiry date. No new tokens will be issued after the termination effective date.

Section 8
Representations & Warranties

8.1 FitOS represents: (a) FitOS will maintain 99% uptime measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows notified 48 hours in advance; (b) FitOS has full authority to enter into this Agreement.

8.2 School represents: (a) School has full legal authority to enter into this Agreement; (b) School will accurately report enrollment numbers; (c) School will not represent to students that FitOS is School's proprietary software.

Section 9
Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages arising from this Agreement. FitOS's total liability for any claim shall not exceed the fees paid by School in the three (3) months preceding the claim.

Section 10
General

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.

10.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions.

10.3 Amendments. Amendments must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

10.4 Severability. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
