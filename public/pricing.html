Questions

Frequently asked

What happens after month 3? +
After your 3 free months of managed hosting, you have two paths: continue on $7/mo and we keep running it for you, or self-host it yourself for free — clone the repo, deploy to your own Render account, done. We'll email you before anything changes. No surprise charges, no lock-in.
Can I self-host for free? +
Yes. FitOS is MIT licensed — git clone, set your environment variables, and deploy anywhere that runs Node.js. Render, Fly.io, Railway, your own VPS — all work. The only requirement is a Postgres database (Neon free tier works fine) and a Google service account for Sheets access. Full guide at github.com/Polsia-Inc/fitos. Self-hosting is free forever with no monthly cost to us.
Do I own my code? +
Yes. FitOS is MIT licensed — you get the full source code, no strings attached. Fork it, extend it, white-label it, sell it. No permission needed. Your client data lives in your Google Sheet (your Google Drive) — not our servers. If FitOS disappeared tomorrow, your data and your code would still be right there.
What is BYOK Anthropic? +
BYOK = Bring Your Own Key. The AI Program Builder uses Anthropic's Claude API. You supply your own Anthropic API key — it's stored AES-256-GCM encrypted in your Google Sheet's Profile tab, never logged, never shared with us. Your AI usage bill goes to Anthropic directly. No markup, no tokens counted against us. Keys are free to create at console.anthropic.com.
How many clients can I have? +
Unlimited. No per-client fee, no tier limits. The only practical ceiling is your Google Sheet's row capacity, which handles thousands of clients comfortably.
Is the 14-day trial the full product? +
Yes — full access to every feature. No feature walls, no "upgrade to unlock." We want you to see the real thing before paying.
What is the $7/mo for? +
$7/mo is for managed cloud hosting — we run FitOS on our infrastructure so you don't have to think about servers, deploys, or uptime. It kicks in month 4 after your lifetime license purchase (first 3 months are free). If you'd rather self-host, it's free forever — deploy to your own Render account or any Node.js host. The $7/mo is 100% optional.
Can I get a refund? +
Yes — 14-day money-back guarantee. If FitOS isn't right for you within 14 days of purchase, email fitos@polsia.app and we'll refund you in full. No questions asked. After 14 days, sales are final (you've had 28+ days of total access between trial and post-purchase).
Do I get updates? +
Yes — lifetime updates are included. As we ship new features (new AI skills, integrations, UI improvements), they're pushed to the repo and auto-deployed if you're on managed hosting. Self-hosters get updates via git pull. There's no separate "update plan" or upgrade pricing. You paid once — you get everything, forever.
I already have a trial — does my data carry over? +
Yes, automatically. Your trial account, Google Sheet, clients, workouts, and check-ins all carry over the moment your payment is verified. There's nothing to migrate. You'll be logged in to the same account you used during the trial.
